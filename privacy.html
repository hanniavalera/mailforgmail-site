Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

MailForGmail ("the app") is a desktop email app that lets you use your own Gmail account. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

What the app accesses

With your permission, granted on Google's sign-in screen, the app uses these Gmail permissions:

  • Read your email (to show your messages, folders and labels).
  • Modify your email (to mark messages read or unread, star, archive, and move them to Trash). The app never permanently deletes email.
  • Compose and send email (to save drafts and send messages you write and confirm).

The app also reads your Google account's email address so it can show which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

  • Your email is transferred only between your computer and Google. The app has no server of its own and does not send your email, contacts or account details to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Your Google sign-in is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. Signing out removes it.
  • To work offline and to make sure nothing you type is lost, the app keeps a local copy of recent message lists and of unsent drafts on your computer. Signing out clears it.
  • Thread summaries and reply suggestions are produced on your computer. No email content is sent to any AI or third-party service.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MailForGmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the email features you use in the app, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not read by any person.

Removing access

Sign out in the app to remove your sign-in from your computer. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for MailForGmail.
