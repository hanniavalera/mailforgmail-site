MailForGmail

MailForGmail is a simple desktop email app for reading, writing and sending Gmail in a calm, uncluttered window. It is made for family members and a small group of people who want an easier way to use their own Gmail account.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account using Google's own sign-in page.
  • The app talks directly from your computer to Gmail. There is no MailForGmail server in between.
  • Your sign-in is stored encrypted on your own computer and can be removed at any time by signing out.
